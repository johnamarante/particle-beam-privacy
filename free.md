# Particle Beam Lite Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

Particle Beam Lite is the ad-supported edition of the game Particle Beam. The game itself runs on your device; the ads are provided by Google AdMob. The paid edition, Particle Beam, has no ads and collects no data (see its own policy at https://johnamarante.github.io/particle-beam-privacy/).

## Data the game collects

None. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases, and it sends nothing about you or your play to us.

## Data collected by our ad provider

To show ads, the app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following, encrypted in transit:

• IP address (which can indicate approximate location)

• Product interactions (for example, ads viewed and tapped)

• Diagnostic information (for example, app performance and crash data)

• Device and account identifiers, including your device's advertising ID

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Where privacy law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Google's own policies apply to this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data stored on your device

Your settings and your level progress and best scores are saved locally on your device so the game can remember them. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Children

Particle Beam Lite is intended for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
