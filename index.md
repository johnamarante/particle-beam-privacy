# Particle Beam Privacy Policy

Effective: November 3, 2026

Particle Beam is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device.

## Data we collect

None. Particle Beam does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. The app does not request network access.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings (such as sound and controls) and your level progress and best scores are saved locally on your device so the game can remember them. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.
